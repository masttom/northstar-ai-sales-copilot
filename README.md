# AI Sales Copilot

A semi-agentic AI Sales Copilot designed to help B2B sales representatives make more reliable customer-facing decisions by combining structured business data with authoritative company knowledge.

The project explores how AI can retrieve, reason over, and combine information from multiple business sources while keeping consequential actions under human control.

## Core Principle

**Read → Retrieve → Reason → Recommend**

The Copilot supports sales decision-making but does not autonomously approve discounts, modify business records, send quotations, or perform other consequential business actions.
