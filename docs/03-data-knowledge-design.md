# AI Sales Copilot --- Data & Knowledge Design

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Design Baseline

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the data and knowledge architecture for the AI
Sales Copilot MVP. It establishes how structured business data,
enterprise documents, document metadata, lifecycle state, cross-system
relationships, synthetic data, and evaluation ground truth are
represented and separated.

The design supports a semi-agentic sales copilot that follows the
operating principle:

**Read → Retrieve → Reason → Recommend**

The Copilot does not become a new source of business truth. It retrieves
authoritative business state and documented knowledge, assembles
evidence, reasons over that evidence, and presents grounded
recommendations or clearly identified information gaps.

## 2. Design Principles

### 2.1 Every Piece of Information Has an Authoritative Home

Information is divided into three categories:

1.  **Structured Business Truth** --- current operational facts stored
    in PostgreSQL, representing logical CRM, ERP, and Product Master
    systems.
2.  **Documented Knowledge** --- company and manufacturer knowledge
    stored in source documents and indexed in Pinecone for retrieval.
3.  **Derived Conclusions** --- conclusions generated by the Copilot
    from retrieved evidence. These are not stored as authoritative
    business facts.

Conversation context and user assertions may provide application
context, but they do not overwrite authoritative business data.

### 2.2 Source of Truth and Retrieval Index Are Different

Original company documents are the authoritative knowledge assets.
Pinecone is a searchable vector representation of those documents, not
the source of truth itself.

PostgreSQL owns mutable operational business state. Pinecone metadata
may duplicate stable identifiers and document properties for retrieval,
but it must not become a second store for mutable operational facts.

### 2.3 Applicability Matters More Than Similarity Alone

A semantically similar document is not automatically valid evidence.
Usable evidence depends on relevance, applicability, authority,
lifecycle state, and the specific business context.

A newer document does not automatically override an older document. A
more specific applicable authoritative rule may override a general rule,
while unresolved conflicts must be surfaced rather than silently
resolved.

### 2.4 Unknown Information and System Failure Are Different

The Copilot distinguishes between information that was successfully
checked but is genuinely unavailable and information that could not be
checked because a source or tool failed.

A source-access failure must never be presented as evidence that the
underlying business fact is unknown.

## 3. Information Architecture

### 3.1 Structured Business Truth

PostgreSQL represents current business state from logical CRM, ERP /
Inventory, Product Master, and commercial-record systems.

Examples include account tier and ownership, opportunity stage and
requested discount, proposed solution products, fulfilled purchase
history, current inventory and incoming stock, and active commercial
entitlement references.

### 3.2 Authoritative and Advisory Knowledge

The document corpus contains:

-   **Manufacturer Technical:** specification sheets, installation
    guides, compatibility matrices, technical bulletins, and technical
    notices.
-   **Northstar Commercial:** discount policies, approval matrices,
    warranty and ExtendedCare policies, promotion rules, and
    account-tier policies.
-   **Customer-Specific:** customer agreements and pricing addenda.
-   **Sales Advisory:** sales playbooks, qualification guides,
    negotiation guides, and solution-selling guidance.

Sales advisory material may support recommendations but must not be
presented with the same authority as technical rules, commercial
policies, customer agreements, or structured operational facts.

### 3.3 Information States

The Copilot uses four information states:

-   **CONFIRMED** --- directly supported by authoritative evidence.
-   **SUPPORTED / INFERRED** --- reasonably supported by available
    evidence but not directly established as a fact.
-   **UNKNOWN** --- required information is genuinely unavailable after
    the relevant source has been checked.
-   **CONFLICT** --- relevant authoritative evidence cannot be
    reconciled confidently.

System availability is tracked separately. A source may be available,
degraded, or failed.

## 4. Structured Data Design

The MVP uses ten relational tables.

### 4.1 Accounts

`account_id`, `account_name`, `segment`, `account_tier`,
`account_status`, `customer_since`, `sales_territory`,
`account_owner_id`.

Discount rules, payment terms, and agreement clauses are not stored
directly on the account.

### 4.2 Sales Users

`sales_user_id`, `name`, `role`, `territory`, `status`.

Commercial authority is defined by policy documents rather than a
duplicated `max_discount` field.

### 4.3 Opportunities

`opportunity_id`, `account_id`, `owner_id`, `opportunity_name`, `stage`,
`estimated_value`, `expected_close_date`, `target_deployment_date`,
`customer_requirements`, `current_discount`, `requested_discount`,
`outstanding_questions`, `last_updated`.

`customer_requirements` remains flexible because requirements differ
across equipment categories and deal types.

### 4.4 Opportunity Solution Lines

`solution_line_id`, `opportunity_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`.

Proposed products are represented relationally rather than as a JSON
solution blob so they can be reliably linked to Product Master and
inventory data.

### 4.5 Orders

`order_id`, `account_id`, `order_date`, `order_status`.

Only fulfilled or completed orders are treated as evidence of purchase
history.

### 4.6 Order Lines

`order_line_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price`,
`installation_location`.

### 4.7 Products

`product_id`, `sku`, `product_name`, `brand`, `category`,
`product_family`, `product_role`, `lifecycle_status`,
`commercial_status`, `list_price`, `launch_date`, `discontinued_date`.

Product Master intentionally remains thin. Detailed capacity, voltage,
dimensions, installation clearance, compatibility, and warranty
conditions belong in technical documents rather than duplicated
structured fields.

### 4.8 Inventory Positions

`product_id`, `warehouse_id`, `on_hand`, `reserved`, `available`,
`last_updated`.

If the ERP provides `available`, the Copilot treats it as authoritative
rather than recalculating it.

### 4.9 Incoming Inventory

`incoming_id`, `product_id`, `warehouse_id`, `quantity`,
`expected_arrival`, `status`.

Status values: `CONFIRMED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `DELAYED`.

### 4.10 Commercial Entitlements

`entitlement_id`, `account_id`, `agreement_id`, `entitlement_type`,
`effective_from`, `effective_to`, `status`.

The structured record establishes which agreement applies. Detailed
agreement clauses remain in the agreement document.

Derived commercial values such as eligible subtotal are not stored as
authoritative opportunity facts. They are calculated from current
solution lines, prices, and applicable commercial rules.

## 5. Knowledge Corpus Design

### 5.1 Corpus Structure

``` text
knowledge/
├── source/
│   ├── product/
│   │   ├── specifications/
│   │   ├── installation-guides/
│   │   ├── compatibility/
│   │   └── bulletins/
│   ├── commercial/
│   │   ├── discount-policies/
│   │   ├── approval-matrices/
│   │   ├── warranty/
│   │   └── promotions/
│   ├── agreements/
│   ├── sales/
│   │   ├── playbooks/
│   │   ├── qualification/
│   │   └── negotiation/
│   └── archive/
└── processed/
```

The corpus deliberately contains overlapping revisions, historical
documents, and unrelated material so retrieval operates in a credible
enterprise knowledge environment rather than a collection created solely
around demo questions.

Old does not automatically mean invalid. A document for a discontinued
product may remain relevant when reasoning about a customer's installed
equipment.

### 5.2 Source Format Strategy

The corpus uses a hybrid PDF and Markdown strategy based on realistic
document origin.

Typical PDF sources include product catalogs, specification sheets,
installation guides, compatibility matrices, technical bulletins, and
customer agreements.

Typical Markdown sources include commercial policies, approval matrices,
warranty and ExtendedCare policies, promotional rules, sales playbooks,
qualification guidance, and negotiation guidance.

Source format and normalized retrieval format are separate concerns.
Original source assets remain intact while the ingestion pipeline
creates normalized machine-readable representations.

### 5.3 Target Corpus Size

The MVP targets approximately 55--70 knowledge documents: roughly 35
product/technical documents, 10 commercial documents, 10 customer
agreements, 6 sales advisory documents, and 5--8 legacy or superseded
documents. Categories may overlap.

## 6. Document Metadata

Core metadata:

``` yaml
document_id:
title:
document_type:
authority_class:
status:
revision:
effective_date:
expiration_date:
supersedes_document_id:
brand:
product_family:
product_ids:
account_id:
agreement_id:
```

Fields may be null when they do not apply.

Controlled document types include `SPECIFICATION_SHEET`,
`INSTALLATION_GUIDE`, `COMPATIBILITY_MATRIX`, `TECHNICAL_BULLETIN`,
`TECHNICAL_NOTICE`, `DISCOUNT_POLICY`, `APPROVAL_MATRIX`,
`WARRANTY_POLICY`, `PROMOTION_POLICY`, `CUSTOMER_AGREEMENT`,
`PRICING_ADDENDUM`, `SALES_PLAYBOOK`, `QUALIFICATION_GUIDE`, and
`NEGOTIATION_GUIDE`.

Controlled authority classes are `MANUFACTURER_TECHNICAL`,
`NORTHSTAR_COMMERCIAL`, `CUSTOMER_SPECIFIC`, and `SALES_ADVISORY`. These
describe the nature of authority, not a universal ranking.

Business objects and documents use separate identifiers. For example:

``` text
agreement_id: AGR-2026-017
document_id:  DOC-AGR-0017
```

This allows one agreement to be represented by multiple documents
without conflating the business object with its documentary
representation.

Product-specific documents may reference `brand`, `product_family`, and
`product_ids`. Customer-specific documents may reference `account_id`
and `agreement_id`. Broad policies are not forced to enumerate hundreds
of accounts or products; detailed conditional applicability remains in
document content where appropriate.

## 7. Document Lifecycle

Document lifecycle uses:

-   `CURRENT`
-   `SUPERSEDED`
-   `RETIRED`

Expiration is an applicability concern expressed through
`effective_date` and `expiration_date`, rather than a document-version
status.

This allows an expired customer agreement to remain the current
authoritative documentary representation of that historical agreement
without implying that its commercial terms remain currently applicable.

`revision` is represented as a string. `supersedes_document_id` creates
explicit document lineage when one revision replaces another.

Supersession does not imply that every statement in the older document
became false. It means the older document should not be treated as
current authority where a newer applicable version governs.

A technical bulletin may introduce a specific condition without
superseding an entire installation guide. Therefore, the system follows
**specific applicable authoritative evidence over general evidence**,
rather than **newest document always wins**.

## 8. Cross-System Relationships

Canonical identifiers such as `account_id`, `product_id`,
`agreement_id`, `opportunity_id`, and `sales_user_id` connect structured
business context with document knowledge.

Relevant stable identifiers may be duplicated into document or vector
metadata to support filtering and retrieval.

Mutable operational facts must remain in their authoritative structured
source. Pinecone metadata must not become a duplicate store for current
account tier, inventory quantity, opportunity stage, requested discount,
or sales ownership.

**Canonical business IDs may be duplicated into document/vector metadata
for linking and retrieval, but mutable operational business facts remain
in their authoritative structured source.**

General company knowledge does not belong to a specific opportunity.
Opportunities provide runtime context that identifies relevant
customers, products, requirements, commercial conditions, and questions.

Typical evidence assembly:

``` text
Structured Business Context
        ↓
Canonical Products / Account / Agreement
        ↓
Relevant Document Retrieval
        ↓
Structured Facts + Document Evidence
        ↓
Evidence Assembly
        ↓
Copilot Reasoning
```

## 9. Data & Knowledge Lifecycle

### 9.1 Structured Data

Human-readable seed files are the canonical synthetic dataset artifacts:

``` text
data/seed/
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
PostgreSQL
        ↓
Bounded Business-Data Tools
        ↓
Copilot
```

The Copilot does not read CSV seed files directly at runtime. Seed files
support inspectability, version control, reproducibility, and database
recreation.

### 9.2 Knowledge

``` text
Source Document
PDF / Markdown
        ↓
Document Registration
        ↓
Metadata Validation
        ↓
Extraction
        ↓
Normalization
        ↓
Chunking
        ↓
Embedding
        ↓
Pinecone
```

Original source documents remain unchanged. Processed representations
may be stored separately for ingestion and debugging.

When a new revision replaces an older document, the previous revision
becomes `SUPERSEDED`, the new revision becomes `CURRENT`, lineage is
recorded, and affected knowledge is reprocessed and re-indexed.

Operational changes such as inventory quantity, opportunity stage, or
account ownership update PostgreSQL only and do not require document
re-embedding.

Incomplete or failed document extraction must not be indexed as valid
knowledge. Runtime source failures must be communicated as system-state
problems rather than converted into `UNKNOWN` business facts.

## 10. Pinecone Representation

Document-level metadata is inherited by indexed chunks where relevant.

Example:

``` yaml
chunk_id: DOC-TECH-0048-C003
document_id: DOC-TECH-0048
document_type: INSTALLATION_GUIDE
authority_class: MANUFACTURER_TECHNICAL
status: CURRENT
revision: "5"
product_ids:
  - PRD-OV660
```

This preserves provenance while enabling metadata-aware retrieval.

Detailed choices such as embedding model, chunk size, retrieval top-K,
reranking, and query decomposition are deferred to the AI Retrieval &
Reasoning Design.

## 11. Synthetic Dataset Strategy

### 11.1 Company Scale and Representative Dataset

Northstar Commercial Solutions represents approximately 300 active SKUs
and 600 active B2B accounts company-wide. The MVP uses a representative
scoped subset rather than reproducing the entire operational universe.

The working dataset is designed to be large enough to create realistic
cross-system relationships, background context, and retrieval noise
while remaining practical for a portfolio-scale implementation. It
includes products across active, phase-out, and legacy lifecycle states;
customers across multiple account tiers and segments; sales users;
opportunities at different stages; purchase history; inventory
positions; incoming stock; and commercial entitlements.

Exact record counts are implementation targets rather than architectural
requirements.

### 11.2 Curated Core and Constrained Background Data

A curated subset of accounts, opportunities, products, purchase
histories, agreements, inventory conditions, and knowledge documents
provides deterministic high-value scenarios for retrieval and reasoning
evaluation.

The remaining dataset is generated under controlled business constraints
to provide realistic background context and retrieval noise. Important
products and scenario relationships are manually curated where
consistency matters, while background data is generated under
constraints rather than unrestricted randomness.

Constraints cover brands, categories, lifecycle states, product roles,
price bands, account tiers, territories, owners, referential integrity,
opportunity stages, orders, and inventory relationships.

A fixed generation seed ensures reproducibility.

### 11.3 Controlled Noise

The dataset contains plausible distractors such as related products,
alternative product families, unrelated customer histories, historical
documents, and superseded revisions.

Controlled noise makes retrieval and entity resolution meaningful
without deliberately corrupting the dataset.

### 11.4 Dataset Validation

Validation covers relational integrity and business consistency,
including:

-   valid cross-table references;
-   lifecycle and date consistency;
-   valid fulfilled-order history;
-   inventory consistency;
-   entitlement-to-document relationships;
-   curated scenario invariants.

Critical knowledge documents such as commercial policies, compatibility
rules, technical bulletins, warranty rules, and customer agreements are
curated rather than generated without control.

### 11.5 Pricing Calibration

Exact product prices, opportunity values, and commercial discount
thresholds are not locked at this stage.

They will be calibrated by defining realistic category-specific price
bands, generating or curating product prices, inspecting representative
opportunity values, calibrating commercial thresholds, and then locking
final commercial-policy figures.

## 12. Evaluation Isolation

Evaluation ground truth is strictly separated from runtime data and
knowledge.

``` text
data/evaluation/
├── test_cases.json
├── expected_outcomes.json
├── compatibility_ground_truth.json
└── commercial_ground_truth.json
```

The runtime Copilot has no access to these files.

Evaluation follows:

``` text
Define Business World
        ↓
Curate / Generate Dataset
        ↓
Validate Internal Consistency
        ↓
Freeze Dataset Version
        ↓
Derive Expected Outcomes
        ↓
Create Evaluation Ground Truth
        ↓
Run Copilot
        ↓
Compare Actual vs Expected
```

Evaluation artifacts test whether retrieval and reasoning reach expected
conclusions from available business data and company knowledge. They
must never be indexed into Pinecone or exposed through runtime
business-data tools.

## 13. Response Support Requirements

The architecture must support responses that identify, when relevant:

-   decision state;
-   conclusion;
-   supporting evidence;
-   information gaps;
-   why missing information matters;
-   required next information or verification;
-   source provenance;
-   customer-facing commitments that should not yet be made.

When evidence is incomplete, the Copilot should tell the sales rep what
must be verified before a reliable customer-facing statement can be
made.

## 14. Design Boundary

This design intentionally does not define the embedding model, chunk
size or overlap, retrieval top-K, reranking strategy, query
decomposition, detailed metadata-filtering logic, evidence requirements
by interaction moment, reasoning prompts, final response schema,
workflow decomposition, or physical database indexes.

Those decisions belong to the AI Retrieval & Reasoning Design or later
implementation design.

The Data & Knowledge Design establishes the information model, ownership
boundaries, lifecycle, linking strategy, synthetic dataset approach, and
evaluation separation required for those later decisions.
